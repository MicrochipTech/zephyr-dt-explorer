## Notes on the design

- `vendor`: `soc.yml` in the RTOS4 workspace (Zephyr v4.3.0) has no vendor key for `atmel/sam0`;
  `board.yml` has `vendor: atmel`. So the board's vendor is used first, then a `vendor` key on the
  SoC's family entry or top level of `soc.yml` (checked by test with a synthetic file).
- The board is located through `CMakeCache.txt` `BOARD_DIR` first, then by searching the
  `boards/` trees of the workspace and modules. Without `ZEPHYR_BASE` there is no workspace to
  search, so `board_yml` and `vendor` stay `None` (checked by test).
- `zephyr_modules.txt` lines are parsed as `"name":"path":"cmake path"`; paths are normalised with
  `os.path.normpath`, since lab1 has a `apps/lab1/../../modules/led` entry.
