## Notes

- Counts match the prototype numbers exactly; the prototype was not rerun (no difference to compare).
- Acceptance 5 shows one hit: `dt_explorer/core/plugins.py:84`, a pre-existing comment example
  (`# lower case with dot, e.g. [".pcbdoc"]`) in the BoardReader protocol. It is outside the P7.1
  write scope and was left unchanged; the new `core/board.py` has no hits. Suggest changing the
  example to `[".ext"]` in a later card.
- Footprints carry 20 extra unnumbered pads named "0" per header (no net); they are kept as pads
  (they are part of the 1251/1181 counts). Consumers must ignore pad "0" when tracing.
- Units: BoardNetlist is in mm (design file: mil). Pad layer "all" = through hole.
- Silkscreen texts: 1600 (E54) and 1973 (SG41); layers top_silk/bottom_silk/other.
- Skipped in the full suite: `test_conftest.py` needs `DTX_MAIN_WS` (not available before P6.1).
