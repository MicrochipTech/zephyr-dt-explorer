Design notes:
- MCU = component whose pad numbers (without pads numbered "0") cover >= 90 % of a DeviceModel package's
  positions and that has <= 2 pads outside the package (SG41 CUlt: exposed pad "129"). Xpro: U200 vs TQFP128
  (the EDBG chip U800 has 100 pads and does not fit); SG41 CUlt: U401 vs TQFP128 of PIC32CX1025SG41128
  (U303, LQFP-100, does not fit).
- Tracing as in the prototype (2-pad R/J/JP/SJ/FB parts, depth 2, never supply rails, nets with > 4 pads
  are not walked when passed through). The ID pin (role ID) is not reported as a gap.
- HC-6: `reviewed: builder` only if the silkscreen text fits the connector type's name pattern, the detection
  score >= 0.5, GND/VCC pins are supply nets, and >= 75 % of the signal pins reach the MCU. Otherwise `suggested`
  with the reason in the YAML.
